Who We Are

Our Mission & Vision

Founded on the belief that every person deserves a church home, Your Church has been serving this community for over 25 years. We are a multigenerational, Spirit-filled congregation committed to love, unity, and impact.

Our vision is a community transformed by the gospel — one family, one life, one neighborhood at a time.

📖

Biblical Teaching

Every message rooted in the truth of God's Word, practical and applicable to your life.

🤝

Community Impact

Active in local schools, food pantries, and neighborhood development throughout the year.

🙌

Vibrant Worship

Spirit-led praise and worship that touches hearts and shifts atmospheres.

What We Believe

Statement of Faith

Core Beliefs

  • We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • We believe in the divine inspiration and authority of the Holy Scriptures.
  • We believe in the virgin birth, sinless life, atoning death, and bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ.
  • We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone.
  • We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit and the gifts of the Spirit in operation today.
  • We believe in water baptism by immersion as a public declaration of faith.
  • We believe in the second coming of Jesus Christ.

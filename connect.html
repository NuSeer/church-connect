Background push notifications

Checking device support…

Install Church Connect and allow notifications. On iPhone, add it to the Home Screen before enabling push.

Step by step: turn on notifications
  1. iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, choose Add to Home Screen, then open Church Connect from your Home Screen.
  2. Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open My Profile and tap Enable notifications.
  4. When your phone asks, tap Allow.
  5. If it says notifications are blocked, open your phone Settings, find Church Connect, and turn Notifications on.
